Language-Agnostic Architecture

Designed for scale. The engine standardizes security checks across any codebase, utilizing your choice of localized or external AI models.

1. Target Codebase Mount

Mount any enterprise repository via Docker volume. The engine parses the raw AST (Abstract Syntax Tree) regardless of the underlying programming language.

2. High-Speed Static Engine

Semgrep rapidly executes hundreds of custom DPDP YAML rules across millions of lines of code in milliseconds, acting as a broad filter for potential privacy violations.

3. BYO-LLM Evaluator

Connect to an air-gapped Local Ollama instance or External APIs (OpenAI/Azure). The LLM reads the business logic around flagged AST nodes to confidently filter out false positives.

4. Unified Dashboard

Results are consolidated into a Streamlit UI, providing audit trails, violation severity reports, and one-click CSV/PDF export functionality.

Seamless CI/CD Integration

Shift-left security. The compliance engine hooks directly into your enterprise GitHub Actions pipelines, automatically annotating pull requests with DPDP violations before code is ever merged into production.

DPDP Act Breakdown & Penalties

Translating the legal rights and obligations of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (2023) into automatable codebase checks.

Section 3

Territorial Scope

Applies to digital personal data processed within India or outside India if offering goods/services to Indian residents.

Section 5

Itemized Notice

Fiduciaries must provide clear, itemized notice of data collected and its purpose before seeking consent.

Section 6 & 7

Consent & Legitimate Use

Scans for data processing functions that fail to explicitly verify boolean consent flags prior to execution.

Section 8 Penalty

₹250 Crores Fine

Data minimization & security. The engine detects unencrypted PII (Aadhaar/PAN) to proactively prevent breaches.

Section 9 Penalty

₹200 Crores Fine

Children's Data rules. The engine flags behavioral tracking algorithms that lack strict age verification barriers (<18).

Section 10

Significant Fiduciaries

Additional obligations (DPO, Data Auditors) for fiduciaries handling large data volumes or high-risk profiling.

Section 11

Right to Access

Data Principals have the absolute right to request a summary of personal data being processed by the fiduciary.

Section 12

Right to Erasure

Identifies instances of non-compliant "soft deletion" when an absolute data purge is legally mandated.

Section 13 & 15

Grievance & Duties

Right to readily available grievance redressal mechanisms, paired with the duty not to file false grievances.

Section 16

Cross-Border Transfers

Analyzes API client payloads to prevent sensitive data objects from being transmitted to negative-listed jurisdictions.

Section 17

Exemptions

State security, legal compliance, and research exemptions applied to specific data processing pipelines.

Appeals & Admin

Data Protection Board

The regulatory authority investigating breaches, with TDSAT acting as the official Appellate Tribunal for disputes.
